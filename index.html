How I present design ideas

A simple explanation framework for interviews and conversations. It describes the design approach, not completed professional commissions.

  1. Purpose: Who will use this space, and what does it need?
  2. Layout: How will people move through it?
  3. Style: Which materials, colors and mood fit?
  4. Furniture: What seating, storage and lighting are practical?
  5. Presentation: Show visual concept, trade-offs and next steps.
